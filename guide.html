GL Linen Guide

Cotswold Retreats · how to use the linen app

Most days you only touch two things: Bag In (count the dirty linen you're sending back) and Alerts (anything unusual). Clean linen going out is now mostly automatic — you don't log it.

Your daily jobs

1

Count the dirty linen

once you've stripped & cleaned a lodge

Go to DAILY OPS › BAG IN

  1. Tap the lodge you've just done. Lodges still waiting to be counted show a small amber ⚠ count?
  2. For every item, put in how many dirty pieces are going back to GL — type the number or use the − / + buttons.
  3. Choose your name under “Counted by”.
  4. Tap ✓ Submit Count.
  5. Repeat for every lodge showing ⚠ count?
If a red “no delivery recorded” warning pops up It just means no clean bag went out for that lodge recently. If you know that's correct, carry on. If clean linen did go out, close it and tell the office — don't force it.
2

Clean linen going out — leave it alone

the app does this for you now

Go to DAILY OPS › DELIVERY

Each morning the app automatically makes up the day's out-bags. A lodge already sorted shows a bright blue ⚡ AUTO ✓.

Do not tap an ⚡ AUTO ✓ lodge It's already logged. Tapping it again would double the count. If you tap one by accident, the app shows a message and stops you — just close it.

Only use Delivery to log a bag the app didn't create (rare). A plain chip with no badge, where a bag really is going out → tap it, adjust the quantities, confirm.

3

Anything unusual

no-shows, cancellations, bags not needed

Go to DAILY OPS › ALERTS — your “needs attention” list.

A bag isn't needed (guest didn't show, or the booking cancelled)? Find the lodge under “Today's Delivery Bags” and tap ↩ Not needed. It then asks: “Is there still dirty linen to collect?”

  • Yes — the clean linen goes back on the shelf, but we still expect the dirty bag. The lodge stays on your ⚠ count? list — count the dirty as normal.
  • No — nothing more to do for that lodge today.

“Uncounted Dirty Linen” lower down = lodges that checked out but you haven't counted yet. Work through them.

The golden rules

Keep these three in mind
  • One tap per bag. Never log the same bag twice — that's what throws the stock off.
  • Dirty coming BACK = Bag In. Clean going OUT = Delivery (and Delivery is mostly automatic).
  • Not sure? Don't guess — message Lee. A quick question is easier to sort than a wrong number.

What the colours mean

⚡ AUTO ✓Already done for you. The clean out-bag is logged automatically — don't tap it.
📦 baggedA clean bag was made up recently for this lodge.
⚠ count?Still needs a dirty count. This is your to-do list on the Bag In screen.
red numbersBelow zero / short. The office keeps an eye on these — you don't need to fix them.

The full picture of the app for office use — every area, how the automatic logging works, and a quick table for the tricky cases that have been tripping people up.

The three main areas

Across the top: DAILY OPS, STOCK, and ADMIN. Staff live in Daily Ops; Stock and Admin are for the office.

📋 DAILY OPS

Sub-tabs: Bag In · Delivery · Alerts · Forecast
  • Bag In — count dirty linen coming back from a cleaned lodge (Item / Expected / Actual / Status), pick a name, submit. Has a “Phantom checkout? Dismiss” option for a checkout that isn't a real booking.
  • Delivery — clean linen going out. Now mostly auto-logged (see below). Chips show ⚡ AUTO ✓, 📦 bagged, or plain, plus the lodge's next checkout day.
  • Alerts — the day's exceptions (detailed below).
  • Forecast — the GL schedule (High season = Mon · Wed · Fri, Low season = Mon · Thu) and a 7-day checkout forecast.

⚡ How auto-logging works

The first time anyone opens or refreshes the app each day, it reads that day's checkout list and automatically creates the clean-linen out-bags — a full standard set per lodge — deducts them from shelf stock, and flags each one ⚡ AUTO ✓. There's no button to press.

This is what stops the old problem of nobody logging the out-bags. If a staff member taps a chip that's already auto-logged, the app blocks it with a message so the bag can't be counted twice.

🔔 Alerts, in detail

  • Unconfirmed Stock Outs — checked out today but no bag out recorded. Tap a lodge to confirm its delivery went out (logs a full set). A bag marked “Not needed” never appears here.
  • Today's Delivery Bags — every out-bag for today, each with an ⚡ AUTO badge and a ↩ Not needed button.
  • ↩ Not needed asks “Is there still dirty linen to collect?” — Yes returns the clean to the shelf but keeps the dirty bag-in expected (still shows ⚠ count?); No is a full no-show and clears the lodge for the day.
  • Uncounted Dirty Linen — checked out but no dirty count yet; each has a Defer option to push to tomorrow.
  • Monday reconciliation — a reminder to do a physical stock count if it's been over a week.

📦 STOCK

Sub-tabs: Warehouse · GL Flow · GL Summary · Daily Report
  • Warehouse — current shelf stock per item. Stock Check records a physical count; ± Adjust Stock makes a manual correction. Red negatives mean linen was sent out before the inbound GL delivery was counted in — they correct once that delivery is logged on GL Flow.
  • GL Flow — the washing pipeline with GL. Log a New Collection (dirty we sent to GL) and a New Delivery (clean GL sent back), each as Received / Was Sent / Diff.
  • GL Summary — running totals of what's with GL.
  • Daily Report — a day's activity summary.

⚙️ ADMIN

Sub-tabs: History · Weekly Summary · Lodge Setup · Pending Returns
  • History — every logged entry, filterable by Out Short Over OK and by date. Entries can be edited or marked returned-unused. Includes Dismissed Checkouts (phantom bookings) with an Undismiss button.
  • Weekly Summary — totals for the week.
  • Lodge Setup — the standard linen set (par) for each lodge.
  • Pending Returns — bags out that are still awaiting their dirty return.
